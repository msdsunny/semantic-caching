⚠️ The Core Problem: Same Meaning, Different Length

Both "price" and "What is the full price of the service you offer to customers?" mean the same thing. But a longer sentence creates a vector with a larger magnitude (longer arrow). Euclidean sees them as far apart. Cosine sees them as nearly identical.
